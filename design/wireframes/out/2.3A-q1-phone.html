Read this first

  1. Matching is by keyword over node labels: a seedling-size claim worded unusually is missed, and a few natural (unplanted) seedlings are included where the paper does not say they were planted.
  2. Nursery fertilization and fertilization at planting are different practices; the question names the first, but most of the corpus is about the second. They are kept apart.
  3. 'Competing vegetation (more of it)' and 'Vegetation control & release' describe the same factor from opposite ends. They are kept apart rather than flipping one, so each claim keeps its own wording.
  4. Growth pools height, diameter and biomass. A treatment can raise one and not another; open the claims.
